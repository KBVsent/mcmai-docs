# maimai DX Top Score Analysis

McMai turns your synchronized play records into a top-score summary and offers related views for Best 40, AP results, play count, and friend comparisons. Before querying, [update your data and select the correct region](/en/guide/score-tracking).

## How to query

1. Update your records using the method for your region.
2. Confirm that CN, JP, or INTL is selected.
3. Send `/b50`; append `#username` to view another player when they allow it.

## Best 50

Query your Best 50 scores (top 35 old-version charts + top 15 new-version charts).

**Command:** `/b50`

Add `#username` to query another player's data (requires their permission).

**Examples:**

| Command |
|---------|
| `/b50` |
| `/b50 #SomeUser` |

::: tip What the image shows
- Player info and play stats, plus your Rating distribution and grade / combo / sync / DX star breakdowns.
- **Rating trend** (JP / INTL only): the summary bar shows your Rating over the last 30 days. See [`/ralog`](/en/commands/score#rating-history) for the full history.
- **Chart Affinity**: with enough scores, the analysis panel shows a radar of your relative performance across chart types and marks your relative weak spots. See [Chart Analysis & Practice](/en/commands/analysis#chart-affinity). It's hidden when there aren't enough scores; send `/practice` for calibration charts.
:::

::: tip Theoretical B50
Curious how high Rating goes if every chart were maxed? Send `/b50 #intlmaxscore` for the INTL theoretical B50, `/b50 #jpmaxscore` for JP, or `/b50 #maxscore` for CN. These are rebuilt automatically whenever the song database updates.
:::

## PK B50

Face off against another player on the charts you've both played and compare Rating.

**Command:** `/pk50 [#username]`

- Specify an opponent: `/pk50 #Sakura`
- No argument: randomly match a player on your server who shares at least 10 played charts with you (only from the current group when sent in a group), preferring players within 100 Rating of you

**Examples:**

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/pk50 #Sakura` | PK against Sakura |
| `/pk50` | Random match |

**Rules:**

1. **Chart set**: from the charts you've both played (UTAGE excluded), the two players take turns picking their own highest-RA chart, up to 35 old-version + 15 new-version charts. Each side brings half of the set from its own strengths, so it doesn't matter who starts the PK.
2. **Verdict**: total Rating on the chart set decides the winner; if tied, the number of charts won decides. Each chart is decided by RA first, then achievement rate if RA is equal.
3. A PK can't start with fewer than 10 shared charts.
4. **Cross-server PK**: if the two players are on different servers, each chart's constant is taken from JP, then INTL, then CN, in that order.

::: tip What the result image shows
- Both nameplates (in-game name frame, dan, and class), the score, and each player's own B50 Rating (for reference only, not used for the verdict).
- A side-by-side comparison of achievement, grade, and RA for every chart, with a per-chart RA difference chart and the chart win tally at the bottom.
- If a player hid their nickname in [Profile Display Settings](/en/commands/settings#profile-display-settings), it's hidden on the image too.
:::

## Update B50 Data

Sync your latest B50 data from maimai NET to local cache.

**Command:** `/update`

::: tip CN Users — Update via QR Code
CN users can use the "Player QR Code" feature from the 舞萌 | 中二 WeChat official account. Long-press the QR code, copy the `SGWCMAID` string, and send it to the Bot to trigger a CN B50 data update.
:::
