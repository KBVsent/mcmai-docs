# maimai DX Top Score Analysis

McMai turns your synchronized play records into a top-score summary and offers related views for Best 40, AP results, play count, and friend comparisons. Before querying, [update your data and select the correct region](/en/guide/score-tracking).

## How to query

1. Update your records using the method for your region.
2. Confirm that the China, Japan, or International Server is selected.
3. Send `/b50`; append `#username` to view another player when they allow it.

## Best 50

Query your Best 50 scores (top 35 old-version charts + top 15 new-version charts).

**Command:** `/b50`

Add `#username` to query another player's data (requires their permission).

**Examples:**

| Command |
|---------|
| `/b50` |
| `/b50 #SomeUser` |

::: tip What the image shows
- Player info and play stats, plus your Rating distribution and grade / combo / sync / DX star breakdowns.
- **Rating trend** (on the China Server, requires NET updates): the summary bar shows your Rating over the last 30 days. See [`/ralog`](/en/commands/score#rating-history) for the full history.
- **Chart Affinity**: with enough scores, the analysis panel shows a radar of your relative performance across chart types and marks your relative weak spots. See [Chart Analysis & Practice](/en/commands/analysis#chart-affinity). It's hidden when there aren't enough scores; send `/practice` for calibration charts.
:::

::: tip Theoretical B50
Curious how high Rating goes if every chart were maxed? Send `/b50 #intlmaxscore` for the International Server theoretical B50, `/b50 #jpmaxscore` for the Japan Server, or `/b50 #maxscore` for the China Server. These are rebuilt automatically whenever the song database updates.
:::

## PC50

Query your top 50 most-played songs.

::: info Requires play counts
- **China Server**: play counts come only from MAID updates, and the current account must be the MAID account (switch with [`/aime`](/en/commands/account#aime)). Data from NET WeChat authorization and LXNS does not include play counts.
- **Japan and International Servers**: requires a bound SEGA ID (the International Server can also use a [sign-in token](/en/commands/account#intl-token)). The query signs in to your account to read play counts, grouped by difficulty, counting up to the 20 most-played charts per difficulty. The current account must be one updated through the SEGA account; bookmarklet data can't be used.
- **Custom data source**: available when the uploaded scores include [`play_count`](/en/guide/custom-data-source#scores).
:::

**Command:** `/pc50`

On the China Server and custom data sources, add `#username` to query another player's data (requires their permission); on the Japan and International Servers you can only query yourself.

## PK B50

Face off against another player on the charts you've both played and compare Rating.

**Command:** `/pk50 [#username]`

- Specify an opponent: `/pk50 #Sakura`
- No argument: randomly match a player on your server who shares at least 10 played charts with you (only from the current group when sent in a group), preferring players within 100 Rating of you

**Examples:**

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/pk50 #Sakura` | PK against Sakura |
| `/pk50` | Random match |

**Rules:**

1. **Chart set**: from the charts you've both played (UTAGE excluded), the two players take turns picking their own highest-RA chart, up to 35 old-version + 15 new-version charts. Each side brings half of the set from its own strengths, so it doesn't matter who starts the PK.
2. **Verdict**: total Rating on the chart set decides the winner; if tied, the number of charts won decides. Each chart is decided by RA first, then achievement rate if RA is equal.
3. A PK can't start with fewer than 10 shared charts.
4. **Cross-server PK**: if the two players are on different servers, each chart's constant is taken from the Japan Server, then the International Server, then the China Server, in that order.

::: tip What the result image shows
- Both nameplates (in-game name frame, dan, and class), the score, and each player's own B50 Rating (for reference only, not used for the verdict).
- A side-by-side comparison of achievement, grade, and RA for every chart, with a per-chart RA difference chart and the chart win tally at the bottom.
- If a player hid their nickname in [Profile Display Settings](/en/commands/settings#profile-display-settings), it's hidden on the image too.
:::

## Update B50 Data

Sync your latest B50 data from maimai NET to local cache. With a SEGA account or LXNS linked, querying your own scores also [updates automatically](/en/commands/account#auto-update).

**Command:** `/update`

::: tip China Server Users
China Server scores are not pulled with `/update`. If you have set up [NET WeChat authorization](/en/guide/cn-net-update), open the McMai NET entry in WeChat to update; if you use a MAID, send a new MAID to the Bot. To get a MAID, use the "Player QR Code" feature from the 舞萌 | 中二 WeChat official account. Long-press the QR code, copy the `SGWCMAID` string, and send it to the Bot to trigger a China Server B50 data update. When the current account is an [LXNS](/en/commands/account#lxns) account, `/update` syncs your scores from LXNS immediately.
:::

::: warning Do not sign in to NET during updates
When Japan or International Server scores are updated through a SEGA account, do not sign in to maimai DX NET manually while the update is queued or running. After the update finishes, recent play records continue to be fetched in the background, so also stay signed out for the next few minutes.
:::
